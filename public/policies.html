Customer care & policies

Please review the terms below before placing an order.

Orders & payment

Orders are subject to stock confirmation. Cash on delivery or bank transfer may be offered where available. Any bank transfer must be verified by OPTOLINE before an order is marked paid. Card payments are not active unless a supported payment gateway is shown at checkout.

Shipping & delivery

Delivery areas, charges and estimated timelines are confirmed by the store team. Delivery estimates may vary by destination, product availability and prescription processing.

Returns & exchanges

Contact OPTOLINE before returning an item. Final terms for unused goods, defects, hygiene-sealed contact lenses and custom prescription lenses must be confirmed in the store's signed-off policy.

Prescription & contact lenses

Prescription details must be current and verified by qualified optical staff. Contact lenses require appropriate professional fitting and hygiene guidance. This website is not a substitute for an eye examination.

Privacy & customer data

Order and chat details are used to process customer enquiries and purchases. Prescription attachments may contain sensitive information and must be accessed only by authorized staff, retained only as long as necessary and protected under applicable privacy requirements.

Virtual Try-On

Virtual Try-On provides an approximate visual overlay only. It does not measure facial dimensions, pupillary distance or optical fit, and cannot guarantee how a frame will look or fit in person.

Business owner action required: Before public launch, have the final delivery, payment, cancellation, refund, warranty, privacy and prescription-data policies reviewed for your actual business and applicable local law. Replace any incomplete terms with approved details.
